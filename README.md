# Rob Rainer

**Managing Director & Principal Engineer, [Control Lattice Systems](https://controllattice.com)**  
Controls systems · Digital twins · Scientific instrumentation · AI-assisted engineering

I’m a controls and systems engineering leader with 20+ years of experience spanning national laboratories, accelerator operations, X-ray instrumentation, EPICS-based controls, precision motion, technical leadership, virtualized infrastructure, and practical AI applications.

My work focuses on making complex systems easier to observe, troubleshoot, operate, and improve.

- **[Same Frequency](https://samefrequencydating.com)** — A neurodivergent-first connection platform built around relationship compatibility, privacy, and a short path off the app.
